Bridge-Building Robot

An autonomous-ish robot that constructs its own bridge to cross a gap.

Course: ENGS 76, Machine Engineering, Dartmouth Tools: SOLIDWORKS, CAM, CNC mill/lathe, 3D printing
  • Mechanism Design
  • Machining
  • Competition

Overview

For Dartmouth's Machine Engineering course, my team designed and built a robot that constructs a bridge across a gap and drives itself across it, competing head-to-head against other teams' robots on speed and reliability. It was an incredible learning experience — a few rapid design sprints, several full redesigns, and a lot of iteration under a hard deadline.

Approach

One of the core subsystems was a threaded-rod arm-lifter that raised and positioned the bridge segments with a precise, repeatable lead-screw actuator. We iterated through several versions of the arm and drive mechanism in CAD before committing to a final design, machining and printing the parts in-house.
