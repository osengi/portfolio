Kangaroo Belt Emergency Seatbelt System

Auto-locking, auto-retracting stretcher strap for ambulance use.

Overview

During a gap year before Dartmouth, I completed an EMT course and worked as a first responder in Cambridge, MA. On calls, I kept running into the same frustration: the straps used to secure patients to backboards were slow and finicky to buckle in emergency scenarios where seconds can make big differences in patient outcomes. When an intro engineering design course gave me the chance to pick a societal problem to solve, I brought this one to the team.

Approach

We redesigned the standard, manual backboard strap to be a locking, retractable, mechanical system — in essence a portable, modular car seatbelt — that mounts directly on the backboard for fast, reliable deployment with minimal fumbling. I led the design of the auto-locking, auto-retracting mechanism and used FEA data as well as physical Instron tensile testing to validate our system against real strength requirements.

Gallery

Results

The final design lowered patient stretcher securing time by 66% compared to the standard strap-and-buckle setup in testing, while meeting DOT Title 49 strength requirements. The team was interested enough in the result that we looked seriously at what it would take to commercialize it.
