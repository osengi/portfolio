Port-A-Dock

An auto-locking strap to keep rowing shells from floating away from the dock.

Overview

For ENGS 75, Dartmouth's product design course, I ended up in a group filled with fellow rowers. As rowers, we'd all dealt with boats drifting off of our dock while we go to get oars or water during practice. To fix this, we designed Port-A-Dock: a strapping mechanism that secures boats to a dock so they can't float away, with a cam-based, auto-locking mechanism that needs almost no thought or effort from the person tying up.

Approach

We iterated through several strap and locking geometries in CAD before landing on a design that could be rigged one-handed and would auto-lock under load without extra steps. We built physical prototypes and tested them directly on the dock with our practice boats.

Gallery

Results

Our prototype reliably held boats against the dock and auto-locked with ease. Our testing also revealed that the product was helpful in aiding beginners or rowers with diminished mobility who might be more likely to flip off the dock while getting in a shell.
