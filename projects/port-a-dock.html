Port-A-Dock

An auto-locking strap to keep rowing shells from floating away from the dock.

Overview

For ENGS 75, Dartmouth's product design course, I ended up in a group filled with fellow rowers. As rowers, we'd all dealt with boats drifting off of our dock while we go to get oars or water bottles before launching. To fix this, we designed Port-A-Dock: a strap mechanism that secures boats to rowing docks so they can't float away. Our product has a cam-based, auto-locking mechanism that needs almost no thought or effort from the rower so that they can focus on other pre-launch tasks.

Approach

We iterated through several strap and locking geometries in CAD before landing on a design that could be rigged one-handed and would auto-lock under load without extra steps. We built physical prototypes and tested them directly on the dock with our practice boats.

Gallery

Results

Our prototype reliably held boats against the dock and auto-locked with ease. Our testing also revealed a new use-case: it was helpful in aiding beginners or rowers with diminished mobility who have trouble keeping the shell stable while getting in before launch.
