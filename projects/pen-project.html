Pen Machining Project

A bolt-action pen, machined from scratch.

Type: Personal Project Tools: CNC lathe, CNC mill, rotary table
  • Machining
  • CNC Lathe/Mill
  • OD Threading

Overview

A personal machining project at Dartmouth's Thayer School Machine Shop: a bolt-action pen, machined entirely from steel and sized to take standard Waterman ink cartridges. It's a small part with a lot packed into it — a threaded, twist-to-fire bolt mechanism, tight tolerances between mating parts, and a finish that has to feel good in hand, not just look right on paper.

Approach

The build combined mill and lathe operations, including OD threading and rotary table work to cut the bolt-action flutes. Getting the sliding and threaded fits right took a few iterations on tolerancing. Once the steel version worked, I made additional versions in copper, titanium, and stainless steel to see how each material machined and finished differently.

Gallery

Results

A fully functional, machined-from-scratch bolt-action pen — and a good excuse to get comfortable with threading and rotary table setups I later relied on for other projects.
